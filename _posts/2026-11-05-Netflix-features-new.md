Netflix is a subscription streaming app that lets you discover and watch shows, films, and other entertainment.
Netflix suits viewers who want one place to choose entertainment at home, while travelling, or during a break. The app brings together global and local titles for people with different interests and viewing habits. It also supports a personal viewing experience when more than one person uses the same account. Before you download Netflix, remember that watching its catalog requires an active membership plan.

Netflix features
Browse different types of entertainment
Netflix lets you browse TV series, films, documentaries, anime, live events, podcasts, and mobile games in one app. You can explore categories or look for a specific title when you already know what you want to watch. New titles are added regularly, so the available selection can change over time.
Find titles based on your viewing
Netflix uses your ratings and viewing activity to suggest series, films, and other content you may want to try. You can also check areas such as Trending Now, previews, and the upcoming clips feed. This can help when you are unsure what to watch next.
Continue watching across supported devices
Netflix saves your viewing progress when you are signed in, so you can return to a title from the point where you stopped. For example, you can begin an episode on your Android phone and continue it later on another supported connected device. Your account keeps this progress linked to your profile.
Download selected titles for offline viewing
You can download selected movies and episodes to your Android device before a trip, commute, or time without reliable coverage. Afterwards, you can watch the saved content without an internet connection. Download availability depends on the title, so some items in the Netflix app may not offer this option.
Save content in My Netflix and My List
Netflix gives you places to keep titles and viewing activity easy to find. Use My List to save films or series you plan to watch later, then open My Netflix to view favorites, memorable moments, and other saved content. As a result, you do not need to search for the same titles each time.

Netflix for family viewing
Create separate profiles for children
Netflix allows account holders to create kids profiles for younger viewers. You can set maturity restrictions, block specific titles, and protect parental controls with a PIN. Each profile can also have its own viewing activity and recommendations, which helps keep suggestions separate for different family members.

Netflix membership details
Netflix is a paid subscription service billed monthly. You can choose a plan, change to a different plan, or cancel online when needed. There are no long term contracts or cancellation fees stated for the membership options, although available plans may vary by location.
